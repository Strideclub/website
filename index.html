(01) The next chapter

From Prater to Mariahilfer Straße.

We built our crew outdoors at Trendsportzentrum Prater: sleds on turf, rowers in the sun and a lot of laughing between rounds. Now we're bringing that energy inside, all year round, in the heart of Vienna.

Same people, same mindset. More space, more lanes and a studio built for one thing: HYROX training.

(02) The session

Built for race day. Made for every day.

Coach-led HYROX sessions: running and functional stations like sled push, sled pull, rowing and lunges, scaled to your level.

(Four lanes)

Room for everyone.

Enough space and equipment for the whole group. Sled push and sled pull in every session, without waiting for your turn.

(Face to face)

You see the crew. The crew sees you.

Every station faces the floor (except the SkiErg). A quick fistbump, a loud "let's go", and everyone else's effort pulls yours up.

(Light & sound)

High energy from the first minute.

Light show, loud music and coaches who push you. You'll feel it the moment you walk in.

(Screens run the workout)

Your coach coaches. Not the clock.

Timer, stations and every transition run on two big screens. So the coach can focus on you: your technique, your questions, your push.

(03) Effort over ego

Every level. One crew.

HYROX is for everyone. It scales to your pace and your weights.

At STRIDE, everyone respects everyone who shows up to grow, no matter how fast. A little challenge now and then? Sure, because it pushes you. Who wins doesn't matter.

Nervous before your first session? That's fine. We'll show you around, explain everything and make sure it turns into excitement.

(04) Your fixed point

“Community”

Every Thursday. The two of us. STRIDE.

Life is busy, and seeing friends regularly takes planning. Make training the plan: same day, same crew, ten minutes of chatting afterwards. Your evening stays free for everything else.

(07) FAQ

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. HYROX scales to every level: you choose your pace and your weights. Our coaches show you everything in your first session.

Do I need to be racing HYROX?

No. Every STRIDE session is HYROX training, a mix of running and functional stations like sled push, rowing and lunges. Whether you have a race in the calendar or just want to get fitter with good people, you're in the right place.

When do you open?

Soon. Opening dates go out to our newsletter and on Instagram first.

How much does it cost?

Prices come with the PreSale. Sign up and you'll get them as soon as it starts.

What about the outdoor sessions in the Prater?

They're coming back. Our outdoor club returns to Trendsportzentrum Prater in spring 2027 (April/May).
